# Document Prioritization

Tags: @docs
Category: Increased Output & Efficiency
Description: The aim of this prompt is to categorize and prioritize work. By summarizing and labeling recently edited documents, individuals can focus on high-priority tasks for maximum output and efficiency.
Google Bard Extensions Prompt: Summarize the most recently edited files in @docs this week. Generate a report of ‘High Priority', 'Medium Priority', or 'Low Priority' based on their current status and deadlines mentioned within. Make recommendations for completing high-priority tasks first.
